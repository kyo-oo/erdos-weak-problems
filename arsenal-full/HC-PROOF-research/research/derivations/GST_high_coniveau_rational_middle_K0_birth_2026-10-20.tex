\begin{abstract}
An unconditional construction is provided for the \emph{topological}
perfect-\(K\) source of every prescribed rational Hodge class on an
arbitrary smooth projective original variety. We combine it with the
previous GST all-channel rational ambient blowup and show that any
prescribed original rational Hodge class can be transported faithfully
to a middle Hodge class on a smooth projective rational variety
with \emph{arbitrarily large prescribed absolute geometric coniveau}.
Every middle rational Hodge class of the chosen rational world has
that coniveau. The topological source comes with an exact
Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch Todd correction, and its algebraizability
is equivalent to original Hodge algebraicity. These are genuine
geometric reductions and source objects; the required universal
algebraization theorem is not proved.
\end{abstract}

\section{A geometrically explicit high-coniveau support}
For a smooth projective variety \(Y\), let
\(N^kH^n(Y,\Q)\) be geometric coniveau, defined using
closed algebraic supports of codimension at least \(k\).

We use the actual exceptional convention
\[
 E=\mathbb P_{\mathrm{lines}}(N),\qquad
 \mathcal O_E(-1)\hookrightarrow q^*N,\qquad
 N_{E/Y}=\mathcal O_E(-1).
\]
Equivalently, these are quotient lines of \(N^\vee\).
Global generation of \(N\) does not imply global
generation of this \(\mathcal O_E(1)\).
Choose an ample line bundle \(\mathcal L\) on \(X\),
put \(h=c_1(\mathcal L)\), and choose \(a\gg0\)
so that \(N^\vee\otimes\mathcal L^a\) and
\(\mathcal L^a\) are globally generated.
The genuine divisor classes
\[
 A=\xi+a q^*h,\qquad B=a q^*h
\]
are then basepoint-free. In the actual Chow ring,
\[
 \xi^r=(A-B)^r
  =\sum_{i=0}^{r}(-1)^i{r\choose i}A^{r-i}B^i.
\]
For \(i\le\min(r,d)\), first cut \(X\) by \(i\)
general members of \(|\mathcal L^a|\), pull the
smooth intersection back to \(E\), then cut by
\(r-i\) general members of \(|A|\).
Bertini gives a smooth complete intersection
\(i_{r,i}:T_{r,i}\hookrightarrow E\) of codimension
\(r\), or the empty set, with
\([T_{r,i}]=A^{r-i}B^i\).
Terms \(i>d\) vanish. For \(r=0\), take \(T_{0,0}=E\).
The maps \(u_{r,i}=j\circ i_{r,i}\) give the exact
finite Gysin expression
\[
 \boxed{\beta_\alpha=
  \sum_{i=0}^{\min(r,d)}(-1)^i{r\choose i}
  (u_{r,i})_*\bigl((q\circ i_{r,i})^*\alpha\bigr).}
 \tag{2.1}
\]
Their finite union is an actual algebraic support of
codimension at least \(r+1=M-p\) in \(Y\).
This proves the same geometric support statement
without assuming \(\alpha\) algebraic or changing
the exceptional normal-line convention.
For a second explicit construction with ONE smooth
support carrying all grades, see
\texttt{GST\_middle\_Euler\_arithmetic\_normalization\_2026-10-20.tex},
Section 7.

\begin{proof}
Equation (2.1) supplies a finite Gysin sum supported

The algebraicity defect is nevertheless

\section{An exact GST middle-world intersection detector}
There is a second completely geometric invariant on the
rational world \(Y\): the cup-product intersection matrix
of all the transplanted exceptional Hodge channels.
This is especially useful for GST's rational Gram-dual
primitive and source-detection calculations.

For \(\alpha\in\Hdg^p(X)_{\Q}\) and
\(\gamma\in\Hdg^q(X)_{\Q}\), write
\(\beta_{p,\alpha}=j_*(\xi^{M-p-1}q^*\alpha)\)
and \(\beta_{q,\gamma}=j_*(\xi^{M-q-1}q^*\gamma)\).
\begin{theorem}[Exact cross-channel GST intersection kernel]
With the Segre convention of the previous GST
all-channel return,
\[
 \boxed{
 \int_Y\beta_{p,\alpha}\smile\beta_{q,\gamma}
 =\begin{cases}
 -\int_X\alpha\smile\gamma\smile
       s_{d-p-q}(N_{X/\mathbb P^{2M}}),
       &p+q\le d,\\
 0,&p+q>d.
 \end{cases}}
 \tag{3.1}
\]
In particular, whenever \(p+q=d\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \int_Y\beta_{p,\alpha}\beta_{d-p,\gamma}
 =-\int_X\alpha\gamma.}
 \tag{3.2}
\]
If \(d=2p\), the original middle Hodge
intersection form is transported by an exact
\emph{anti-isometry} into the middle Hodge
intersection form of the smooth rational world.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exceptional divisor self-intersection is
\(j^*j_*(v)=-\xi v\). By projection formula,
\[
 \int_Y\beta_{p,\alpha}\beta_{q,\gamma}
  =-\int_E
  \xi^{(M-p-1)+(M-q-1)+1}q^*(\alpha\gamma)
  =-\int_E\xi^{2M-p-q-1}q^*(\alpha\gamma).
\]
Since the projective fibers of \(q\) have
dimension \(c-1=2M-d-1\),
the pushforward is \(s_{d-p-q}(N)\)
if \(p+q\le d\). If \(p+q>d\),
\(\alpha\gamma\) has degree exceeding
the top cohomology of \(X\), so the
integral vanishes. In the complementary
case \(p+q=d\), \(s_0=1\). The
anti-isometry assertion follows immediately.
\end{proof}

\section{Unconditional topological \(K\)-theory source}
Write \(KU^0(E)_{\Q}=KU^0(E)\otimes\Q\).
Because \(E\) is smooth complex projective,
it is a finite CW complex, and the rational
topological Chern character is an isomorphism
\[
 \ch_{\mathrm{top}}:KU^0(E)_{\Q}
  \xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }
  \bigoplus_{a\ge0}H^{2a}(E,\Q).
 \tag{3.1}
\]
The exceptional normal line is
\(N_{E/Y}=\mathcal O_E(-1)\), with
\(c_1=-\xi\). Its Todd class is the exact
truncated rational series
\[
 \td(\mathcal O_E(-1))
  =\frac{\xi}{e^\xi-1}
  =1-\frac{\xi}{2}+\frac{\xi^2}{12}
   -\frac{\xi^4}{720}+\cdots .
 \tag{3.2}
\]
All power series are truncated at the
finite complex dimension of \(E\).
Define an \emph{explicitly determined}
rational topological virtual bundle class
\[
 \boxed{
 u_\alpha=
 \ch_{\mathrm{top}}^{-1}
 \Big(q^*\alpha\smile\xi^{M-p-1}
     \smile\td(\mathcal O_E(-1))\Big)
 \in KU^0(E)_{\Q}.} \tag{3.3}
\]
Unlike an alleged algebraic perfect complex,
the object (3.3) \emph{always exists}, with
no algebraic preimage of \(\alpha\) assumed.

Let \(j_!^{\mathrm{top}}:KU^0(E)_{\Q}
\to KU^0(Y)_{\Q}\) be the complex topological
Gysin pushforward. Topological
Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch says
\[
 \ch_Y(j_!^{\mathrm{top}}u)
  =j_*\left(\ch_E(u)
     \smile\td(N_{E/Y})^{-1}\right).
 \tag{3.4}
\]
Define \(v_\alpha=j_!^{\mathrm{top}}u_\alpha\).
Substituting (3.3) gives the \emph{exact
pure middle-degree formula}
\[
 \boxed{\ch_Y(v_\alpha)=
 j_*(q^*\alpha\,\xi^{M-p-1})
 =\beta_\alpha\quad
 \text{with all other Chern character
 degrees zero}.} \tag{3.5}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact algebraization criterion for the unconditional source]
The following statements are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item \(\alpha\) is the canonical Betti class
       of a rational algebraic codimension-\(p\)
       cycle on \(X\);
 \item \(u_\alpha\) belongs to the rational
       image of algebraic \(K_0(E)\) inside
       \(KU^0(E)_{\Q}\);
 \item \(v_\alpha\) belongs to the rational
       image of algebraic \(K_0(Y)\) inside
       \(KU^0(Y)_{\Q}\);
 \item the pure middle Hodge class
       \(\beta_\alpha\) is algebraic on \(Y\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The equivalence between (1) and (4) is
the previously proved Chern--Segre
exceptional return.
For (1) implies (2), if \(\alpha\)
has a rational Chow representative,
then \(q^*\alpha\,\xi^{M-p-1}
\td(\mathcal O_E(-1))\) is a finite sum
of rational algebraic Chow classes on \(E\).
The rational Chern character
\(K_0^{\mathrm{alg}}(E)_{\Q}
 \to\bigoplus_a\CH^a(E)_{\Q}\)
is an isomorphism for smooth projective
\(E\), so an actual rational algebraic
\(K_0\) class has the prescribed Chern
character, and by rational injectivity
of \(\ch_{\mathrm{top}}\) it equals \(u_\alpha\).
For (2) implies (1), take
the degree \(M-1\) component of its
Chern character, which is exactly
\(\xi^{M-p-1}q^*\alpha\) since
the Todd class starts in degree zero.
Multiply by \(\xi^{M-d+p}\) and push
forward along \(q\):
\[
 q_*\left(
  \xi^{M-d+p}\,
  [\ch_E(u_\alpha)]_{M-1}
  \right)
  =q_*(\xi^{2M-d-1}q^*\alpha)
  =\alpha.
 \tag{4.1}
\]
The left side is a canonical Betti class
of a rational Chow cycle because the
assumed \(K\)-class is algebraic.
The implication (2) to (3) follows
by algebraic regular-immersion pushforward
and its topological comparison.
The implication (3) to (4) follows
from (3.5): the degree-\(M\)
Chern character of an algebraic
\(K_0\) class is a rational algebraic
Chow class, and its cycle class is
\(\beta_\alpha\).
Finally (4) to (1) follows by
the original-\(X\) Chern--Segre return.
\end{proof}
The theorem gives a genuine topological
source for \emph{every} target without
circularity. The transition from that
source to an \emph{algebraic} perfect
complex is the still-unproved geometric
problem: it is precisely the Hodge
conjecture in the transported component.

\section{A closed GST \(K\)-theory polynomial: the only nonalgebraic input}
The topological Chern-character construction admits a more
explicit \emph{native bundle ring} formula, avoiding any
unspecified inverse on the new exceptional world.
Define the original rational topological class
\[
 a_\alpha=\ch_{X,\mathrm{top}}^{-1}(\alpha)
 \in KU^0(X)_\Q,\qquad
 \lambda=[\mathcal O_E(1)]-1
 \in KU^0(E)_\Q.
\]
Here \(\ch(a_\alpha)=\alpha\) is \emph{pure}
in degree \(2p\), by the rational topological
Chern-character isomorphism. On a finite-dimensional
smooth complex projective \(E\), the
augmentation element \(\lambda\) is nilpotent
over \(\Q\) (equivalently, its rational
Chern character has strictly positive degree).
Hence the logarithm
\[
 L=\log(1+\lambda)
  =\lambda-\tfrac12\lambda^2+
   \tfrac13\lambda^3-\cdots
\]
is a finite rational polynomial in
\(\lambda\), and so is
\(g(\lambda)=L/\lambda=
1-\lambda/2+\lambda^2/3-\cdots\).
For the exceptional exponent \(r=M-p-1\),
define the \emph{universal algebraic line-bundle
word}
\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi_r(\lambda)
 =\lambda^r\,g(\lambda)^{r+1}
 =\lambda^r
 \left(\frac{\log(1+\lambda)}{\lambda}\right)^{r+1}.}
 \tag{5.1}
\]
The notation with division is a power-series
abbreviation for a finite polynomial; it
does not require inverting the generally
nonunit element \(\lambda\).

\begin{theorem}[Exact finite bundle-word source formula]
The canonical unconditional topological source
constructed in (3.3) is given by the
\emph{explicit rational \(K\)-ring operation}
\[
 \boxed{
  u_\alpha=q^*a_\alpha\cdot\Phi_{M-p-1}(\lambda)
 \in KU^0(E)_\Q.}
 \tag{5.2}
\]
In particular, \(\Phi_r(\lambda)\) belongs to the image
of \emph{algebraic} \(K_0(E)_\Q\)
for every \(r\), and the only possibly
nonalgebraic input in this GST source is
\(a_\alpha\) on the original \(X\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Applying the Chern character to the finite
formal expression gives
\(\ch(\lambda)=e^\xi-1\) and
\(\ch(L)=\log(e^\xi)=\xi\).
Thus
\[
 \ch(\Phi_r(\lambda))
 =\frac{\xi^{r+1}}{e^\xi-1}
 =\xi^r\td(\mathcal O_E(-1)).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \ch(q^*a_\alpha\Phi_r(\lambda))
 =q^*\alpha\,\xi^r\td(\mathcal O_E(-1)),
\]
which characterizes the unique rational
topological class \(u_\alpha\) from (3.3).
All rational powers of \(\lambda\) come from
the actual algebraic line bundle
\(\mathcal O_E(1)\), so the finite
rational polynomial \(\Phi_r\) is
algebraic independently of \(\alpha\).
\end{proof}

\paragraph{No-go boundary for purely formal GST blowup \(K_0\) upgrades.}
The actual blowup and relative-Todd machinery

\section{A concrete GST one-step support-descent gate}
High absolute coniveau alone does not solve
the rational Hodge problem, because
\(\beta_\alpha\in N^{M-p}\) still
has a codimension-\(p\) source.
The useful geometric endpoint is
a \emph{smooth divisor-source correspondence}
in codimension \(M-1\).

\begin{theorem}[Genuine divisor-source algebraicity criterion]
Let \(Y\) be smooth projective complex,
and let \(\beta\in\Hdg^M(Y)_\Q\).
Suppose there exist smooth projective
varieties \(T_1,\ldots,T_s\), proper
algebraic maps \(f_a:T_a\to Y\)
that shift cohomology by codimension
\(M-1\), and rational classes
\(\gamma_a\in H^2(T_a,\Q)\) satisfying
\[
 \boxed{\beta=\sum_{a=1}^s(f_a)_*\gamma_a.}
 \tag{7.1}
\]
The \(\gamma_a\) need not initially be
declared Hodge classes. Then there are
\emph{actual rational algebraic divisors}
\(D_a\in\CH^1(T_a)_\Q\)
with
\[
 \boxed{\beta=\cl_Y^M
              \left(\sum_a(f_a)_*D_a\right).}
 \tag{7.2}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The direct-sum Gysin map
\(\bigoplus_a H^2(T_a,\Q)(-(M-1))
 \to H^{2M}(Y,\Q)\)
is a morphism of polarizable rational
Hodge structures. Its rational image is a
Hodge substructure, and a surjection onto
that image splits as a rational Hodge
morphism by semisimplicity of the
category of polarizable pure rational
Hodge structures. Since \(\beta\) is
a rational Hodge class of type
\((M,M)\) in this image,
it has a rational preimage of type
\((1,1)\) in the direct sum of
degree-two source cohomologies.
By the divisor/Lefschetz (1,1)
theorem, those source components
are rational classes of actual
divisors \(D_a\). Proper
Gysin/nativity of canonical cycle
class gives (7.2).
\end{proof}

\section{An exact relative-support algebraicity test}
We can turn the preceding sufficient criterion
